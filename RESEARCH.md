## Carried into UsedBook.com

- One dominant search with Buy / Sell / Value modes
- ISBN validation + deep links into 13 buy and 10 sell destinations
- Book page with editions/ISBN picker and any-edition vs exact-ISBN toggle
- Six tools: value, condition, first edition, ISBN, reseller profit, collection
- Lead engine: collection offers (3-step), valuation, book-finding, buyer partners, exit-intent and sticky CTAs
- Store & book-sale finder with geolocation and listing submissions
- Glossary, 21 guides, video library, newsletter, contest, support tiers, careers, advertise
- Weekly auto-updated trending + daily rotating subject shelf
- Trust stack: editorial policy, affiliate disclosure, trademark disclosure, privacy with AdSense disclosures
