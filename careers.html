How we work

  • Remote-first. Work from anywhere; local roles are tied to where you live.
  • Paid per project or per result. Rates agreed up front in writing.
  • Honesty over hype. We tell readers when their books aren’t valuable. We expect the same integrity.
  • Credit where due. Writers and creators are credited unless they prefer otherwise.

UsedBook.com welcomes applicants of every background. We assess work, not credentials.
