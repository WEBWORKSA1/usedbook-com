How to find book sales most people miss

  • Friends of the Library groups run recurring sales, often with a members-only preview the night before. Joining usually costs little and gets you first pick.
  • Bag days on the last day of a sale let you fill a bag for a flat price — ideal for reading copies.
  • University and college sales surface academic and specialist titles that rarely show up at thrift stores.
  • Estate sales listed online often include photos of bookshelves. Zoom in before you go.
  • Church, school and charity fundraisers are usually advertised locally rather than online — check community boards.
