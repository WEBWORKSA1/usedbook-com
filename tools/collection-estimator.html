Why bulk offers look low — and how to raise yours

A bulk buyer has to sort, clean, list, store and ship every book, and many will never sell. Offers therefore reflect the handful of books they expect to profit on, spread across the whole lot. That is why the most effective way to raise an offer is to identify and separate the valuable books first.

  1. Pull anything old, signed, illustrated, in a dust jacket, on local history or in a specialist subject.
  2. Check a sample with the value estimator and price comparison.
  3. Sell current textbooks through buyback — they carry real resale value.
  4. Offer the remainder as a lot, or donate it. Read Inherited a book collection?
