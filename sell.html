    Get more for every book you sell

    • Sell early. Textbook and bestseller prices drop steadily; a quote today is usually higher than the same quote next term.
    • Grade honestly. Buyback services check condition on arrival and can reduce or reject an order. Use the condition grader before you quote.
    • Separate the outliers. Before you sell a box by weight, check anything old, signed, illustrated, local-history or in a dust jacket. Our value estimator shows what to look for.
    • Mind the minimums. Many buyback services have a minimum order value to get free shipping. Group books into one order where possible.
    • Check sold prices, not asking prices. On eBay, filter by “Sold items” to see what buyers actually paid.
